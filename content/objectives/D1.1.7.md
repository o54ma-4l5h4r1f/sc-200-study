## Azure first

- An **alert** is one suspicious signal ("malware found on PC-12"). An **incident** is a bundle of related alerts that tell one attack story. AIR starts from alerts and incidents.
- **Defender XDR** is the Defender portal (`security.microsoft.com`) plus the engine that joins signals from Defender for Endpoint (devices), Defender for Office 365 (email) and Defender for Identity (on-premises Active Directory accounts).
- A **remediation action** is a fix: quarantine a file, stop a process, isolate a device, soft-delete an email.
- **Entra roles** such as **Security Administrator** decide who can configure AIR and who can approve its actions. Defender **unified RBAC** can grant the same rights more narrowly.

New to these? Read the Foundations pages linked above.

## In one sentence

**Automated investigation and response (AIR)** is Defender's built-in robot analyst: when certain alerts fire it investigates the evidence, labels each item *Malicious*, *Suspicious* or *No threats found*, and either fixes the bad items itself or queues the fixes for a human to approve in the **Action center**.

## Why it exists

A SOC drowns in alerts. Most are routine: a known piece of malware on one laptop, a phishing email in three mailboxes. A human analyst follows the same steps every time — check the file, look for it on other devices, quarantine it, close the alert. AIR does those steps automatically, 24 hours a day.

Picture a smoke detector wired to a sprinkler. A small kitchen fire triggers it, the sprinkler fires, and the fire brigade gets a report afterwards. For a bigger fire in the server room, the system asks the building manager before flooding the room. AIR's **automation level** is that "act first or ask first" dial.

## The 2026 change — read this first

==!As of 1 September 2026, AIR in **Defender for Endpoint** no longer runs as a separate investigation experience and can no longer be triggered manually.== Microsoft says its detection and response logic is already inside Defender Antivirus's default protection stack and runs automatically. For an on-demand check, you now **run a full antivirus scan** on the device.

==+AIR for **Defender for Office 365** (email) is unchanged and still available.==

What this means for you, as of 12 November 2026:

- The device-page action **Initiate Automated Investigation** is gone for MDE.
- **Automation levels on device groups still exist.** They still decide whether device remediation is automatic or waits for approval, and attack disruption still reads them (see D1.1.8 and D1.1.9).
- The **Action center** is unchanged. It still holds pending and completed actions for devices, email and identities.
- The training modules and many exam questions were written before this change. Expect questions about the classic MDE investigation flow. Answer them with the documented behaviour below; in the real portal, apply the caveat.

## What actually matters

**1. The investigation flow.** An alert creates an incident. The incident can start an automated investigation. The investigation gives every piece of evidence one of three **verdicts**: ==@Malicious==, ==@Suspicious== or ==@No threats found==. Malicious and suspicious items get **remediation actions**. While it runs, new related alerts join the same investigation. If the bad item turns up on another device, the investigation **expands** to that device. If expansion finds **10 or more devices** from one entity, the expansion itself waits for approval on the **Pending actions** tab. Not every alert triggers an investigation.

**2. AIR works across three products.**

| Entity | Product |
|---|---|
| Devices | Defender for Endpoint |
| On-premises Active Directory users and their behaviour | Defender for Identity |
| Email messages, attachments, URLs | Defender for Office 365 |

**3. Email is different: it never auto-remediates.** Certain Office 365 **alert policies** (in the **Threat management** category) trigger AIR playbooks for email. ==!No remediation action is taken automatically for email and content.== Every email action — **soft delete email messages or clusters**, **block URL (time-of-click)**, **turn off external mail forwarding**, **turn off delegation** — waits in the Action center for approval. AIR for email can also be started manually, for example from **Explorer**.

**4. Automation levels decide device behaviour.** They are set **per device group** (full detail on D1.1.9):

| Level | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| ==+Full - remediate threats automatically== | Malicious items fixed automatically. **Recommended.** Default for tenants created on or after **16 August 2020** with no device groups. |
| Semi - require approval for all folders | Every file remediation waits. Default for tenants created **before** 16 August 2020. |
| Semi - require approval for core folders | Approval only for OS folders such as `\windows\*`. |
| Semi - require approval for non-temp folders | Automatic only in temp/download folders; everything else waits. |
| No automated response | Automated investigation does not run. Not recommended. |

==!Pending actions time out after 7 days. A timed-out action counts as **rejected**.==

**5. The Action center.** One list of remediation actions for devices, email and identities, in the Defender portal.

- **Pending** — actions waiting for a human. Approve or reject one at a time, or several of the same type together (for example *Quarantine file*). You can also approve from inside an incident.
- **History** — the audit log. It holds actions from automated investigations, actions your team approved, manual response actions, **live response** commands and actions by **Defender Antivirus**.
- **Undo** — on the History tab. You can undo: isolate device, contain device, contain user, restrict code execution, quarantine a file, remove a registry key, stop a service, disable a driver, remove a scheduled task. ==!Only Security Administrators and higher can undo.== A quarantined file can be released on many devices at once with **Apply to X more instances**.
- **Action source** column tells you who did it: Manual device action, Manual email action, Automated device action, Automated email action, Advanced hunting action, Explorer action, Manual live response action, Live response action (via API).
- Filters for the past day, week, 30 days or **6 months**. **Export** to CSV.

The home page has an **Automated investigation & response** card with **View pending actions** as a shortcut.

**6. The investigation page.** Open it from an incident's **Investigations** tab. Tabs: **Investigation graph**, **Alerts**, **Devices**, **Evidence**, **Entities**, **Log**, and **Pending actions** (only when something waits). The **unified investigation page** shows devices, email and collaboration content together.

**7. The MDE settings around AIR.** In **Settings** > **Endpoints**:

| Setting | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Automatically resolve alerts** | General > **Advanced features** | Resolves alerts where no threats were found or the threats were remediated. Never overwrites an analyst's manual *In progress* / *Resolved*. The old **Automated Investigation** toggle is gone — it is on by default. |
| **Automation uploads** | Rules > Automation uploads | **File Content Analysis** (upload files/attachments with chosen extensions, such as `exe`, `bat`, for cloud inspection) and **Memory Content Analysis** (upload process memory). |
| **Automation folder exclusions** | Rules > Automation folder exclusions | Folders AIR skips. Narrow each by **extensions** and **file names** so attackers cannot hide in the excluded folder. |
| **Auto remediation** | General > Auto remediation | Quick list of device groups and their remediation level. |

**8. Who can do what.**

| Task | Role |
|---|---|
| Configure AIR | Entra **Security Administrator** (or higher) |
| Approve / reject device actions | Entra Security Administrator; or unified RBAC **Security operations / Security data / Response (manage)**; or MDE RBAC **Active remediation actions** |
| Approve email actions | Security Administrator **and** an Email & collaboration role group with the **Search and Purge** role (by default **Data Investigator** and **Organization Management**) |
| Undo | Security Administrator or higher |

==!Membership in the *Security Administrator role group* in Email & collaboration permissions does not open the Action center.== You need the Entra **Security Administrator** role.

**9. Prerequisites.** Licences: Microsoft 365 E5 or A5, Microsoft 365 E3 with the **Microsoft Defender Suite** add-on, Microsoft 365 A3 with the A5 Security add-on, or Office 365 E5 + EMS E5 + Windows E5. MDE AIR needs **Defender Antivirus** in active **or passive** mode. Windows 10 1709+ and Windows 11; Windows Server 2019+ (2012 R2 / 2016 in preview with the unified agent).

## Where it lives

| Task | Portal | Path |
|---|---|---|
| Approve / undo actions | Defender portal | **Actions & submissions** > **Action center** (`security.microsoft.com/action-center`) |
| Shortcut | Defender portal | Home > **Automated investigation & response** card > **View pending actions** |
| See an investigation | Defender portal | **Incidents & alerts** > **Incidents** > incident > **Investigations** tab |
| Automation level per group | Defender portal | **System** > **Settings** > **Endpoints** > **Permissions** > **Device groups** (column **Remediation level**) |
| Automation uploads / folder exclusions | Defender portal | **System** > **Settings** > **Endpoints** > **Rules** |
| Email alert policies that trigger AIR | Defender portal | **Email & collaboration** > **Policies & rules** > **Alert policy** (`security.microsoft.com/alertpoliciesv2`) |

There is no CLI for this. The Defender for Endpoint API can take device actions; those show as **Live response action** in the Action center.

## Exam traps

- ==!AIR remediates email automatically on full automation.== ==+Email actions always wait for approval.== Automation levels are a **device** setting.
- ==!A pending action left alone eventually runs.== It times out after **7 days** and is treated as **rejected**.
- ==!The Action center is the incident queue.== It lists **actions**, not incidents.
- ==!History only shows AIR actions.== It also shows manual, live response, advanced hunting, Explorer and antivirus actions — check the **Action source** column.
- ==!"No automated response" stops antivirus.== It only stops automated investigation. Antivirus and PUA protection still act.
- "Expansion to more devices is waiting for approval" → the threat was found on **10 or more** devices.
- "Which default level does an old tenant have?" → **Semi - require approval for all folders** (created before 16 August 2020). New tenants: **Full**.
- ==!Search and Purge is enough to approve email actions.== You also need **Security Administrator**.
- After 1 September 2026: ==!"Select Initiate Automated Investigation on the device page"== is no longer how MDE works. ==+Run a full antivirus scan== for on-demand checks. Email AIR is unaffected.

**Two Microsoft sources disagree.** Training unit 4 names the strictest semi level *"Semi - require approval for any remediation"*. The current docs and the attack-disruption settings pane call it *"Semi - require approval for all folders"*, and the device-group wizard shows *"Semi - Approval required for all folders"*. Same setting; the product UI wording wins.

## Skip

- **Skip:** the full remediation-action catalogue for Defender for Office 365 (every email action and its sub-options). Know the four email actions listed above.
- **Skip:** the investigation **Log** tab detail and the per-playbook descriptions. Know the tab names.
- **Skip:** the list of temporary folder paths for *non-temp folders*. Know that "temp and downloads auto-fix, everything else waits".
- **Skip:** training unit 7's long **Submissions** walkthrough (submitting emails, URLs and attachments to Microsoft, throttling limits, grader analysis). It sits in the Action center unit but it is an email-reporting workflow, not AIR.
- **Skip:** the supported-OS build numbers and KB articles for MDE AIR. The feature is now folded into the antivirus stack.
- **Skip:** the "40% more malware removed with full automation" marketing statistic.
- **Skip:** Defender for Business AIR. It is pre-set to full automation and has no device groups in the same sense.
