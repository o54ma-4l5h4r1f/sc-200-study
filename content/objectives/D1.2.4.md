## Azure first

- Microsoft Sentinel bills mainly by **data ingested** (GB per day) into a **Log Analytics workspace**, plus retention beyond what is included. More tables means a bigger bill.
- An **analytics rule** is a detection query. A table that no rule, hunt or workbook ever reads costs money and protects nothing.
- The **Content hub** is where you install Microsoft's ready-made detections (analytics rule templates) and data connectors.
- **MITRE ATT&CK** is a public catalogue of attacker **tactics** (goals, like *Credential Access*) and **techniques** (methods). Sentinel uses it to measure detection coverage.
- **Data collection rules (DCRs)** and **transformations** filter data on the way in, before you pay for storing it.

New to these? Read the Foundations pages linked above.

## In one sentence

SOC optimization is a built-in Sentinel page that, every 24 hours, studies your tables and detections and gives you two kinds of to-do cards: **"you pay for data you don't use"** (data value) and **"you can't see this attack coming"** (coverage).

## Why it exists

Think of a home energy audit. An inspector walks through and says, "You heat a room nobody uses" and "This door has no lock". SOC optimization is that inspector for Sentinel. Without it, a SOC engineer must hand-check which of hundreds of tables any rule reads, and which attacks lack detections. Microsoft automated the audit. The cards are recommendations only. Nothing changes until you act.

## What actually matters

**1. Two families of recommendation.**

| Family | Sub-types | What it looks at | Typical action |
|---|---|---|---|
| ==@Data value== | Unused tables; unused columns (preview) | **Billable tables that ingested data in the last 30 days** — and whether rules or detections used them | Turn on analytics rule templates, **or** move to a cheaper plan/tier, **or** stop ingesting |
| ==@Coverage== | **Threat-based**; **AI MITRE ATT&CK tagging** (preview); **Risk-based** (preview) | Your ingested logs + enabled analytics rules vs what Microsoft research says each attack needs | Turn on templates, connect missing data sources, install a solution |
| **Similar organizations** | — | ML compares you with orgs that have similar ingestion and industry | Connect the data sources they use |

**2. The data value rules, word for word in spirit.**

| Observation | Suggested action |
|---|---|
| Table not used by **analytics rules or detections** in 30 days, but used by workbooks, queries or hunting | Turn on rule templates, **or** move the table to a cheaper plan if eligible |
| Table **not used at all** in 30 days | Turn on rule templates, **or** stop ingestion / remove the table / move to long-term retention |
| Table used **only by Azure Monitor** | Turn on security rules, **or** move it to a **non-security** Log Analytics workspace |
| Unused column (for example `ConditionalAccessPolicies` in `SigninLogs`) | Stop ingesting that column |

SOC optimization **won't** suggest cutting a table chosen for **UEBA** or for **threat intelligence matching analytics**. Before acting, check that the table isn't kept for **compliance**.

**3. Coverage, and the High/Medium/Low indicator.** Threat-based recommendations count your active rules against Microsoft's recommended rules for a scenario (for example *human-operated ransomware*). It shows three cases: *data present but detections missing*, *detections on but data missing*, or *neither*. The Defender portal's overview shows **High** (over **75%** of recommended rules active), **Medium** (**30–74%**) or **Low** (**0–29%**). Drill into a scenario to see spider charts of tactics and techniques, then jump to the **MITRE ATT&CK** page or the **Content hub**. Coverage counts both built-in and your own custom detections.

**4. Managing cards.** New cards are **Active**. You can **Mark as in progress**, **Complete**, **Dismiss** or **Provide feedback**. If Sentinel notices the issue is fixed (for example, a new rule now uses the table), the card is **completed automatically**, and a banner says so. Completed and dismissed cards can be **reactivated**; recalculation takes up to an hour. Recommendations refresh **every 24 hours**. You need only normal **Sentinel roles** — no special role.

**A worked example.** You open **SOC optimization** on Monday. The top metrics show 4 TB ingested over 90 days and **Low** threat coverage. A data value card says `AzureDiagnostics` wasn't used by any analytics rule in 30 days. Select **View details**. The card shows the table's size and cost and offers two paths. **Go to Content Hub** opens the rule templates that would use that table. **Change plan** moves it somewhere cheaper. You pick the templates, enable two rules, and mark the card **In progress** so teammates know. Next day the engine sees rules reading the table and **auto-completes** the card. A second card, *human-operated ransomware*, shows missing detections. **View full threat scenario** lists the gaps by MITRE ATT&CK technique and links straight to the filtered Content hub.

The lesson: a card is never an order. Data value cards can trade security for cost. Check compliance needs before you stop collecting anything.

**5. The cost levers the cards point to.** Optimization is more than the cards. These are the platform settings they lead you to:

| Lever | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Data lake tier** for secondary data | Cheap storage for high-volume, low-detection-value logs (D1.2.2) |
| **Total retention** instead of long hot retention | Keep old data cheaply in the lake; Sentinel includes 90 days hot |
| **Filter transformation** | Drop useless rows at ingestion (for example routine "allow" firewall events) |
| **Split transformation** | Matching rows go to Analytics (and are mirrored to the lake); the rest go to the **lake only**, into a separate table with the ==@`_SPLT`== suffix |
| **DCR event filtering** | Collect only the Windows events you need (D1.3.2) |
| **Commitment tier** | Pay a daily-volume price from **100 GB/day** upward, cheaper than pay-as-you-go. You can raise it any time (that ==!restarts== the 31-day period); lowering or returning to pay-as-you-go waits for the **31-day** commitment to end. Changing it needs **Contributor** or **Owner** on the workspace. |
| **Separate non-security workspace** | Operational data doesn't pay Sentinel prices |
| **Dedicated cluster** | Pools commitment across workspaces ingesting **100 GB/day** or more in one region |

## Exam traps

- "Find tables you pay for but no detection uses." ==+SOC optimization data value recommendations==. Not ==!Workbooks== (they visualize, they don't judge value) and not ==!Usage queries by hand==.
- "Close detection gaps for ransomware using Microsoft's research." ==+Threat-based coverage recommendation==, then **Go to Content Hub**.
- "Table is used by workbooks and hunting but by no analytics rule." Suggested: ==+enable rule templates or move to a cheaper plan==, not delete it outright.
- "Table only used by Azure Monitor." ==+Move it to a non-security workspace.==
- "How often are recommendations recalculated?" ==+Every 24 hours.== Look-back for data value: ==+30 days==, billable tables only.
- "Will it ever recommend dropping a UEBA source table?" ==!No.==
- "Need a special role for SOC optimization?" ==!No== — ==+standard Sentinel roles==.
- "Keep only blocked firewall events in Analytics, archive the rest cheaply." ==+Split transformation== (not filter — a filter deletes the rest).
- "Downgrade a commitment tier today, set 10 days ago." ==!Not yet== — lowering waits for the 31-day period.
- "Where do you see which tables drive ingestion cost, and which connectors are failing?" ==+Data & connectors > Overview== (preview, Defender portal) and **Table insights** on the Tables tab. SOC optimization judges *value*; these show *volume and health*. ==!Table insights is not a billing-grade figure.==

## Skip

- **Skip:** the AI MITRE ATT&CK tagging options (apply to one rule, all rules, or none). Preview; know only that it suggests ATT&CK tags for untagged rules.
- **Skip:** the risk-based categories list (operational, financial, reputational, compliance, legal). Know "risk-based = business-risk scenarios, preview".
- **Skip:** the SOC optimization **API** and the recommendation JSON. Not on the blueprint.
- **Skip:** pre-purchase plans and the pricing examples (dollar amounts, discount percentages). Prices move; the exam doesn't test them.
- **Skip:** the "similar organizations" ML privacy notes (OII, EUII). One line is enough: it compares your ingestion with similar orgs, and not every workspace gets it.
- **Skip:** dedicated-cluster setup. Know the 100 GB/day threshold, nothing more.
