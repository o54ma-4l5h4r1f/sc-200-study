## Azure first

- **MITRE ATT&CK** is a free public catalogue of how real attackers operate. **Tactics** are the *why* (Initial Access, Persistence, Credential Access...). **Techniques** are the *how* (T1078 Valid Accounts, T1110 Brute Force). **Sub-techniques** are finer variants. **Procedures** are one attacker's specific implementation — together, **TTPs**.
- **Analytics rules** (D1.4.3) are Sentinel's detections. Each rule can be **tagged** with the tactics and techniques it catches.
- **Microsoft Sentinel** runs in the Azure portal or the Defender portal. The MITRE page exists in both, under **Threat management**.
- **Licence:** works with an Azure subscription and a Sentinel workspace.

New to these? Read the Foundations pages linked above.

## In one sentence

The **MITRE ATT&CK** page in Sentinel is a heat-map of the ATT&CK matrix that counts how many of your *active* detections cover each technique — and, with **simulated** coverage, how many more you *could* switch on.

## Why it exists

A building manager wants to know which doors have alarms, not just how many alarms exist. Fifty rules that all watch the front door leave the back window open. ATT&CK is the floor plan of every door and window an attacker uses. The MITRE page paints the plan: dark where you're covered, blank where you're blind. That turns "we have 200 rules" into "we have nothing for Lateral Movement".

## What actually matters

1. **Where and what.** Defender portal: `Microsoft Sentinel > Threat management > MITRE ATT&CK`. Azure portal: `Threat management > MITRE ATT&CK (Preview)`. The page is in ==@preview== and aligned to **ATT&CK version 18**. Prerequisites: data connectors and active scheduled/NRT rules.
2. **Current coverage.** By default the matrix counts ==@active scheduled and NRT rules==. Read it with three tools:
   - the **legend** — how many active detections per technique;
   - the **search bar** — find a technique by name or ID (for example `T1078`);
   - **select a technique** — a details pane links to the full MITRE description and to your active items (for example **Hunting queries** jumps to a filtered Hunting page). In the Defender portal the pane also shows *recommended coverage*: active detections and products versus all recommended for that technique.
3. **Simulated coverage.** "Simulated" = detections that are ==@available but not configured==. Pick items in the **Simulated rules** menu (rule templates, hunting queries...) to see what coverage you *would* have. It's planning, not protection.
4. **Threat scenarios (Defender portal).** Toggle **View MITRE by threat scenario** and pick a scenario. The matrix shows only that scenario's techniques. The training unit notes that with a scenario selected, ==!simulated rules are disabled==.
5. **Tagging is what feeds the page.** In the analytics rule wizard **General** tab, the **MITRE ATT&CK** field takes multiple tactics and techniques. They apply to every alert from the rule and to incidents built from those alerts. You can **filter the rule list by technique**. Hunting queries and **bookmarks** carry tactics/techniques too. Rules with no tags still detect — they just ==!don't show on the matrix==.
6. **Custom detections are not on it yet.** Defender custom detections (D1.4.1) have Tactic, Techniques and Sub-techniques fields, but appearing on the MITRE page and linking multiple tactics are both listed as **Planned**.

> **Training vs product.** The training unit says the matrix shows active scheduled, NRT **and anomaly** rules by default. The current product article says **scheduled and NRT**. Go with the product article.

## Where it lives

| Task | Defender portal | Azure portal |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage matrix | `Microsoft Sentinel > Threat management > MITRE ATT&CK` | `Microsoft Sentinel > Threat management > MITRE ATT&CK (Preview)` |
| Tag a rule | `Microsoft Sentinel > Configuration > Analytics` → rule → **General** → MITRE ATT&CK | same under `Configuration > Analytics` |
| Tag a custom detection | `Hunting > Custom detection rules` → wizard → Tactic / Techniques | — |

There's no KQL or CLI for the matrix itself. The tags are rule properties (`-Tactic` on `New-AzSentinelAlertRule`).

## Exam traps

- ==!Simulated coverage means you're protected.== ==+Simulated = available but not enabled.==
- ==!Every rule appears on the matrix.== ==+Only rules tagged with techniques, and by default only active scheduled/NRT rules.==
- ==!Tactics are the how.== ==+Tactics = why (goal); techniques = how.==
- ==!Custom detections already feed the MITRE page.== ==+Planned, not yet.==
- ==!The MITRE page is GA.== ==+Preview.==
- A question asking "show what you'd cover if you enabled every template" wants **simulated coverage**.
