## Azure first

- A **Microsoft 365 tenant** is your organisation's copy of Microsoft 365. Its mailboxes live in **Exchange Online**. Every Defender for Office 365 feature on this page works on those cloud mailboxes only.
- **Defender for Office 365 (MDO)** is the email-and-collaboration member of the Defender XDR family. **Plan 1** detects and blocks. **Plan 2** adds the investigation and response tools on this page. Plan 2 is in Microsoft 365 E5 or sold as an add-on.
- The **Defender portal** (`security.microsoft.com`) is where you work. Email tools sit under **Email & collaboration**; the shared queues sit under **Incidents & alerts** and **Actions & submissions**.
- **Roles** decide who may look and who may act. Reading email metadata, previewing a message and deleting it from mailboxes are three different permissions.

New to these? Read the Foundations pages linked above.

> **Licence-blocked for an Azure-only learner.** MDO needs Exchange Online mailboxes and an MDO licence. A 90-day MDO Plan 2 trial exists, but only inside a Microsoft 365 tenant. Treat the screenshots below as your lab.

## In one sentence

When a bad email gets past the filters, you **find** every copy with **Threat Explorer**, **inspect** one copy on the **email entity page**, **remove** them with **Take action** (or let **AIR** do it), **tell Microsoft** through **Submissions**, and let **automatic attack disruption** contain the account if the email was the start of something bigger.

## Why it exists

Email filters are not perfect. Some malware is new on the day it arrives. Some links are clean at delivery and turned malicious an hour later. Some phish only gets reported when a user clicks **Report**.

So a SOC needs a second chance after delivery. Picture a postal sorting office that has already delivered letters. Later it learns one sender was mailing letter bombs. It needs a register of every letter delivered (Threat Explorer), a way to open one letter safely and study it (email entity page), and a van that collects all copies from every desk (remediation). Defender for Office 365 Plan 2 is that register, workbench and van.

## What actually matters

**1. Threat Explorer vs Real-time detections.** Both are near-real-time email search tools under **Email & collaboration**. ==@Real-time detections== comes with **Plan 1**. ==@Threat Explorer== (labelled just **Explorer** in the menu) comes with **Plan 2**. Explorer has more views, more filters, **saved queries** and the full set of remediation actions. Real-time detections shows malicious mail **at delivery only**. Explorer also shows what happened **after** delivery (ZAP, admin moves). Both look back **30 days**. Both default to **yesterday and today**.

| View | Explorer (P2) | Real-time (P1) | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| All email | default | — | Every inbound, outbound and intra-org message, good and bad |
| Malware | yes | default | Messages with malware |
| Phish | yes | yes | Messages with phishing |
| Campaigns | yes | — | Coordinated phishing/malware campaigns |
| Content malware | yes | yes | Bad files in SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams |
| URL clicks | yes | — | Who clicked which link, and the click verdict |

Each view has the same four parts: **date filter**, **property filters**, a **chart** with pivots, and a **details area** with tabs. Export holds up to **200,000** rows.

**2. The email entity page.** It is the one-message workbench. There is no menu link to it. You reach it with **Open email entity** at the top of the **Email summary panel**, the flyout that opens when you click a message in Explorer, an alert, an incident, Quarantine, Submissions or Advanced hunting. The left pane shows tags (including **Priority account**), **original vs latest delivery location**, **detection technology** and message IDs. The tabs are **Timeline**, **Analysis**, **Attachments**, **URL** and **Similar emails**. **Email preview** and **Download email** need the **Preview** role.

**3. Take action: the remediation actions.** You remediate from Explorer, the entity page, the summary panel or Advanced hunting with the **Take action** wizard.

| Action | What it does | Undo? |
|---|---|---|
| Move to **Junk** / **Inbox** / **Deleted items** | Moves the message | Move it back |
| **Soft delete** | To `Recoverable Items\Deletions`. User and admin can recover. Option **Delete sender's copy** for internal senders | Yes (Move to Inbox) |
| **Hard delete** | Purged. Admin recovers only with single-item recovery | Admin only |
| **Submit to Microsoft for review** | Reports it; can add Tenant Allow/Block List entries | — |
| **Initiate automated investigation** | Starts AIR (Explorer only) | — |
| **Propose remediation** | Two-step approval: creates a soft-delete action that someone else approves | — |

Select **up to 100** messages and every action is available. Select **101 to 200,000** and Explorer offers only **Move or delete** or **Propose remediation**, one or the other. Every direct remediation also raises an alert named **Administrative action submitted by an Administrator**. Only mail in cloud mailboxes can be touched. Mail that was dropped, delivered on-premises or already hard-deleted is **not remediable**.

**4. AIR for email.** Automated investigation and response is Defender's robot analyst. It starts from certain alerts (suspicious email, ZAP, user-reported phish, URL clicks, suspicious mailbox behaviour) or from **Take action** in Explorer. It widens the search, labels what it finds and **queues** fixes such as **Soft delete email/cluster**, **Block URL (time-of-click)**, **Turn off external mail forwarding** and **Turn off delegation**. ==!Those email actions wait for a human by default.== The exception: AIR **auto-approves** its own remediation of malicious **URL and file similarity clusters**. Pending actions **time out after one week**; the investigation then shows **Terminated By System**. You approve them in the investigation's **Pending actions** tab, the incident's **Evidence and response** tab, or the **Action center**. When AIR finds **no threat** (common for user-reported mail) it **closes the investigation and resolves the alert by itself**. MDO AIR still runs as before. Only the Defender for Endpoint (device) flavour stopped running as a separate investigation on **1 September 2026** — see D1.1.7.

![The investigation details page in the Microsoft Defender portal](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-xdr/media/m365d-autoir-results/mtp-air-investdetails.png){An automated investigation's details page — the Pending actions you approve live in tabs like these}

> **Sources disagree.** The training unit *Automate, investigate, and remediate* describes MDO "running auto remediations" — deleting the emails and triggering an Entra password reset. The product docs say email remediation actions ==!aren't taken automatically== and need SecOps approval; only URL/file similarity clusters are auto-approved. ==+Go with the product docs.== The unit shows one tenant's policy-driven outcome, not the default.

**5. Submissions.** Under **Actions & submissions > Submissions** you report **emails, URLs, attachments** and (Plan 2, preview) **Teams messages** to Microsoft as **bad** (*I've confirmed it's a threat* / *It appears suspicious*) or **good** (*I've confirmed it's clean*). The **User reported** tab holds what users reported; from there you **Submit**, **Mark as and notify**, or (Plan 2) **Trigger investigation**. Optional Tenant Allow/Block List entries come with the submission. Block entries default to **30 days**. Allow entries have ==!two different defaults== depending on where you create them: **45 days after last used date** from the Submissions page, but **1 day** from Explorer's **Take action > Submit to Microsoft > Allow messages like this**. You can submit mail up to **30 days** old. If you disagree with Microsoft's verdict you **Dispute** it.

**6. ZAP.** Zero-hour auto purge is the automatic version of remediation. It re-checks mail delivered in the **last 48 hours** when Microsoft's verdicts change, then moves or quarantines it. It is **on by default** for malware, phishing and high-confidence phishing. It also works on Teams chats. In the entity page you see it as a change in **Latest delivery location** and a **ZAP** event on the Timeline.

**7. Automatic attack disruption — the email angle.** You do not run it. Defender correlates email, identity and endpoint signals into one high-confidence incident (99%+ confidence) and contains the compromised **account**, not the email. Typical email-born scenarios are **business email compromise (BEC)** and **adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) phishing** (the attacker proxies the sign-in page and steals the session cookie). Every automatic action can be **undone** by the SOC, and critical accounts can be **excluded**. Actions include **Disable user**, **Suspend user in Entra**, **Revoke user session** and **Contain user**. You spot it by the **Attack Disruption** tag and a **yellow banner** on the incident. MDO's prerequisites: mailboxes in **Exchange Online** and mailbox auditing of `MailItemsAccessed`, `UpdateInboxRules`, `MoveToDeletedItems`, `SoftDelete` and `HardDelete`. Configuration is D1.1.8. Cleaning up after it is D2.2.4.

**8. Who can do what.** This is where marks are lost.

| Task | Email & collaboration role group | Entra role | Defender unified RBAC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Use Explorer, read-only | Security Reader | Security Reader, Global Reader | Email & collaboration metadata (read) |
| Preview / download a message | **Preview** role (Data Investigator, eDiscovery Manager) | — not enough on its own | Email & collaboration content (read) |
| Move / delete from mailboxes | **Search and Purge** role (Data Investigator, Organization Management) | Security Administrator *plus* Search and Purge | Email & collaboration advanced actions (manage) |
| Approve AIR actions | Security Administrator, Security Operator, … *plus* Search and Purge | Security Administrator, Security Operator, … *plus* Search and Purge | Email & collaboration advanced actions (manage) |
| Submit to Microsoft | Security Administrator, Security Reader | Security Administrator, Security Reader | Response (manage) or Security data basics (read) |

The pattern: ==+viewing is cheap, touching mail needs Search and Purge, reading the body needs Preview.== Hard delete under unified RBAC also needs **Email & collaboration content (read)**.

## Where it lives

All in the **Microsoft Defender portal** (`security.microsoft.com`).

| Task | Portal path | Direct link |
|---|---|---|
| Hunt all mail (P2) | Email & collaboration > **Explorer** | `/threatexplorerv3` |
| Hunt malicious mail (P1) | Email & collaboration > **Real-time detections** | `/realtimereportsv3` |
| Saved queries | Email & collaboration > **Threat tracker** | `/threattrackerv2` |
| AIR investigations | Email & collaboration > **Investigations** (also in the incident) | `/airinvestigation` |
| Approve / review actions | Actions & submissions > **Action center** > Pending / History | `/action-center/pending` |
| Report to Microsoft | Actions & submissions > **Submissions** | `/reportsubmission` |
| Alerts and incidents | Incidents & alerts > **Alerts** / **Incidents** | `/alerts`, `/incidents` |
| Quarantine | Email & collaboration > Review > **Quarantine** | `/quarantine` |
| Built-in alert tuning rules | System > Settings > Microsoft Defender XDR > **Alert tuning** | `/securitysettings/defender/alert_suppression` |

Advanced hunting can remediate too. Email tables such as `EmailEvents` carry the `NetworkMessageId` that links back to the entity page.

```kql
EmailEvents
| where Timestamp > ago(7d)
| where SenderFromDomain =~ "contoso-billing.example"
| project Timestamp, RecipientEmailAddress, Subject, DeliveryLocation, NetworkMessageId
```

### Phishing Triage Agent (Security Copilot)

The second mapped unit is about the **Phishing Triage Agent**. It is a Security Copilot agent that classifies **user-reported phish** as True Positive or False Positive, explains why, assigns the alert to itself and tags the incident **Agent**. It needs Security Copilot capacity (SCUs) and MDO Plan 2. Know that it exists and that it ignores alerts hidden by alert tuning. Agentic investigation is D2.1.8's job.

## Exam traps

- ==!Real-time detections can do everything Explorer can.== False. ==+Explorer (Plan 2) adds All email, Campaigns and URL clicks views, saved queries, and the Move/delete, Initiate automated investigation and Propose remediation actions.==
- ==!Security Administrator can delete phishing mail from mailboxes.== Not on its own. ==+Moving or deleting needs the Search and Purge role== (or unified RBAC *Email & collaboration advanced actions (manage)*).
- ==!Security Administrator can preview the message body.== ==+Preview needs the Preview role== (Data Investigator or eDiscovery Manager), or *Email & collaboration content (read)*.
- ==!Soft delete is permanent.== ==+Soft delete moves mail to Recoverable Items\Deletions; the user can still recover it. Hard delete purges it.==
- ==!AIR deletes the phishing email on its own.== ==+AIR queues Soft delete email/cluster for approval.== Only malicious URL/file **similarity clusters** are auto-approved.
- ==!Pending AIR actions wait forever.== ==+They time out after one week.==
- **Analyst without permission wants mail gone** → ==+Propose remediation==: someone with rights approves it in **Action center > Pending**.
- **"Find every copy of this message in all mailboxes"** → Threat Explorer, not the email entity page. **"Why did this one message get delivered?"** → email entity page, **Analysis** tab.
- **Original vs latest delivery location**: original is where the filters put it; latest reflects ZAP and admin moves. ==!User actions are not shown==, so "Inbox" does not prove the user still has it.
- ==!ZAP can clean any mailbox, any age.== ==+Cloud mailboxes only, mail delivered in the last 48 hours.==
- ==!Attack disruption deletes the BEC emails.== ==+It contains the account (disable / suspend / revoke sessions / contain user).== Mailbox cleanup is still your remediation work.
- Submissions vs Tenant Allow/Block List: a **submission** asks Microsoft to re-grade; the **block entry** is your local override. A submission can create one, but they are different things.
- ==!The Phishing Triage Agent closes true phish for you.== ==+It resolves false positives; a True Positive stays open for an analyst.==
